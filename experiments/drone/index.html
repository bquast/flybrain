About this experiment

FlyWire v783 supplies the brain's connections. A bounded rate model propagates activity through that graph. A hand-tuned adapter converts bilateral LPLC2/LC4 activity to steering and speed; it has not been trained.

The ventral nerve cord is not included. Soma-side labels are a coarse routing assumption, not measured visual receptive fields. The expanding-object and dimming benches hold the drone still and feed synthetic retinal frames.

The delay slider adds simulation-time command latency. Measured compute time and simulation speed are shown separately. Setting changes restart the trial for comparison.
