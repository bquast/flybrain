About this experiment

MaleCNS v1.0 supplies connections across the brain, optic lobes and ventral nerve cord. A bounded rate model propagates activity through that graph. A hand-tuned adapter converts bilateral LPLC2/LC4 activity to steering and speed; it has not been trained.

The nerve cord's circuits are simulated; descending and motor activity is monitored. Steering still reads LPLC2/LC4. Soma-side labels (nerve-entry side for sensory cells without a soma label) are a coarse routing assumption, not measured visual receptive fields. The stimulus benches hold the drone still.

The delay slider adds simulation-time command latency. Measured compute time and simulation speed are shown separately. Setting changes restart the trial for comparison.
