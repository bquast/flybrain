What is the fly hearing?

We take the amplitude of audio filtered to 60–1,000 Hz and feed a bounded drive into MaleCNS cells labelled auditory with types JO-A or JO-B: neurons from Johnston's organ in the antennae.

This is an engineered sensory adapter. It does not model air-particle motion, antenna mechanics, subtype frequency tuning, musical understanding or enjoyment. Both JO families receive the same amplitude signal. The direction slider scales left/right input using anatomical side labels.

The shared rate model propagates that drive through the connectome. Connections are anatomical; their dynamics are simplified and uncalibrated. Unlabelled or centre-side cells are excluded from bilateral plots.

Change the stimulus, mute it, or compare Bach with the pulsed tone. Reset starts a fresh trial. CSV records both audio and model clocks, settings, inputs and responses.
